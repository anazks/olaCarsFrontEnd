# Chapter 1: Introduction & System Hierarchy

Welcome to the **OlaCars Application User Manual**. This documentation is designed to help you navigate the system effectively, whether you are an executive analyzing high-level metrics or an operational staff member managing day-to-day vehicle inventory.

This platform is a unified system designed to handle the complete lifecycle of vehicle sales, purchasing, fleet operations, workshop management, and financial accounting. 

---

## 1.1 The Organizational Hierarchy

OlaCars uses a strict, role-based hierarchy to ensure data security and operational efficiency. What you see on your dashboard depends entirely on the role assigned to your account. 

The system hierarchy flows as follows:

1. **Executive / Country Managers**: Highest level of access. Global oversight of all branches, finances, and operations.
2. **Administrative Managers**: Specialized heads of departments (Financial Admin, Operational Admin).
3. **Branch Management**: Leaders responsible for a specific physical branch location.
4. **Branch Staff**: The day-to-day operators divided by their functional duties (Operations vs. Finance).

---

## 1.2 User Role Descriptions & Functions

Below is a breakdown of the default roles within the system and their primary functions. 

### Executive & Country Manager
* **Function**: Strategic oversight and global reporting.
* **Key Capabilities**: 
  * Access to high-level Intelligence Reports and Financial Statements.
  * Visibility across all branches and workshops.
  * Cannot typically alter day-to-day lower-level transactions, but can view all historical data and performance metrics.

### Financial Admin
* **Function**: Overseeing the economic health and accounting backbone of the entire organization.
* **Key Capabilities**:
  * Full access to the General Ledger, Chart of Accounts, and Tax Management.
  * Ability to approve large Purchase Orders and view all Invoices.
  * Management of corporate Bank Accounts and Fixed Assets.

### Operational Admin
* **Function**: Managing the physical assets (vehicles, workshops) and human resources of the organization.
* **Key Capabilities**:
  * Full access to Fleet Management (Vehicles, Drivers, Agreements).
  * Oversight of Workshop operations (Scraps, Write-offs, Accident Reports).
  * Ability to monitor Staff Performance and issue directives globally.

### Branch Manager
* **Function**: Complete oversight of a specific, localized branch.
* **Key Capabilities**:
  * Ability to view and manage both operations and finances *only for their assigned branch*.
  * Responsible for onboarding local staff and managing localized vehicle inventory.

### Branch Finance Staff
* **Function**: Day-to-day accounting for a localized branch.
* **Key Capabilities**:
  * Processing payments received (Collections) and payments made (Bills).
  * Managing localized expenses and generating customer invoices.
  * Cannot view the global General Ledger or global Financial Statements.

### Branch Operations Staff
* **Function**: Day-to-day physical handling of inventory and sales.
* **Key Capabilities**:
  * Inducting new Pending Vehicles into the active fleet.
  * Registering new customers and creating sales orders.
  * Logging daily accident reports or initiating workshop repairs.
